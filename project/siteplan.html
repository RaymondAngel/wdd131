Website Planning Document

Site Name

High Desert Development

The name clearly identifies the company and connects it to the High Desert community it serves. “Development” is broad enough to represent construction, remodeling, utilities, site preparation, and multiple skilled trades while allowing the company to grow.

Existing company domain: highdesertdevelopment.com. The WDD 131 assignment version will first be published through the student’s GitHub Pages repository.

Site Purpose

The website introduces Jerry Heller and High Desert Development as an experienced, client-focused general contractor. Established in 1999, the company serves residential and commercial clients with construction, remodeling, concrete, framing, utility, septic, sewer, and site-preparation work.

The site helps visitors understand the company’s capabilities, view representative project photographs, learn about Jerry’s approach to planning and communication, watch a virtual project tour, and submit details about a potential project.

The assignment website includes Home, About, Contact, and References pages. Services and featured project work are presented as major sections on the Home page.

Scenarios

  • Can High Desert Development coordinate the utility, site, concrete, and construction work involved in my project?
  • Where can I see examples of the company’s residential, commercial, concrete, septic, sewer, and remodeling work?
  • Who is Jerry Heller, and what experience does he bring to my project?
  • How can I contact the company and submit the details of a project inquiry?

Content and Page Structure

Company representative: Jerry Heller, a general contractor with more than 30 years of construction experience.

  • Home: company introduction, responsive hero image, six photograph-based service links, a featured project, virtual-tour link, and contact call to action
  • About: company history, Jerry Heller’s background, client-focused values, communication approach, planning practices, and connection to the High Desert
  • Contact: verified telephone and email links plus a project-inquiry form for the visitor’s name, email, telephone, service, location, and project details
  • References: citations for company information, project media, the official logo, fonts, and the virtual tour

Service Categories

The Home page organizes the company’s capabilities into six visual categories. Each card uses a representative project photograph and leads visitors toward related work.

  • Underground utilities: C-900 water mains, fire mains, hydrants, deep wells, seepage pits, dirt work, and backhoe work
  • Sewer and septic: main sewer-line taps, trenching, plumbing, and 1,000–1,500-gallon septic-system installations
  • Concrete and masonry: residential and commercial design-build concrete, custom stamping, patios, walkways, and block walls
  • Commercial construction: commercial build-outs, storage facilities, and commercial wood framing
  • Custom residential construction: residential framing, custom homes, pool houses, and accessory dwelling units
  • Remodeling: bathroom remodeling, custom showers, fireplaces, and interior improvements

Additional capabilities mentioned by the company include a 2,000-gallon water truck for construction, landscaping, and water-delivery needs.

Dynamic Functionality

JavaScript provides the responsive navigation behavior, remembers the last service category selected by a visitor, preselects that category on the inquiry form, saves a local copy of the inquiry, and displays submission feedback.

The implementation demonstrates multiple functions, DOM selection and modification, event listeners, conditional branching, objects, arrays and array methods, template literals, and localStorage.

Typography

Montserrat

Montserrat is used for navigation, headings, buttons, and short labels. Its strong geometric letterforms support a confident construction brand.

Open Sans

Open Sans is used for paragraphs, lists, forms, and longer descriptions because it remains clear and readable at many screen sizes.
