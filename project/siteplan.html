Website Planning Document

Site Name

High Desert Development

This name clearly identifies the contractor and connects the company to the High Desert community it serves. “Development” is broad enough to represent construction, remodeling, and multiple skilled trades while allowing the company to grow.

Optional domain: highdesertdevelopment.com (availability has not been verified and a domain is not required for this project).

Site Purpose

The website will introduce Jerry Heller and High Desert Development as a dependable full-service contractor. It will explain the company’s utility, concrete, masonry, framing, new construction, and remodeling services; share its values and experience; display completed work through project photos and videos; and provide a contact form so property owners can ask questions or request an estimate.

The completed website is planned to include Home, About Us, Portfolio, Services, and Contact Us pages.

Scenarios

  • Can High Desert Development handle both the utility work and construction involved in my project?
  • Where can I see examples of the contractor’s completed work and workmanship?
  • How can I contact the company to discuss my project and request an estimate?

Planned Content

Company representative: Jerry Heller

The Services page will organize the company’s capabilities into clear groups:

  • Underground utilities: installation of 8-, 10-, and 12-inch water mains, fire mains and hydrants, main sewer line taps, septic systems, deep wells, and seepage pits
  • Concrete and masonry: residential and commercial concrete design-build work, custom concrete stamping, and residential and commercial block walls
  • Construction: commercial build-outs, commercial and residential wood framing, new custom homes, pool houses, and other custom structures
  • Remodeling: bathroom remodels and custom shower installation

The Portfolio page will use optimized project photos and videos, including a virtual project tour on YouTube. Final project images will be selected and added during site development.

Typography

Montserrat

Montserrat will be used for the logo, navigation, headings, buttons, and short labels. Its strong geometric letterforms support a confident construction brand.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, lists, forms, and longer descriptions because it remains clear and readable at many screen sizes.
